## Current research

**Auditing 3D medical vision-language models.**
A question-answering benchmark of 18,691 questions, verified against segmentation masks from 1,228 CT scans (TotalSegmentator), used to audit M3D-LaMed. Next steps are evaluation on Merlin and confidence calibration.

**Does the model read the prior scan?**
A study of visual grounding when a model answers questions by comparing a prior and a current CT scan (3D-RAD). Code: [prior-scan-grounding](https://github.com/Mosharrof8097/prior-scan-grounding)

**Undergraduate thesis: hallucination and recency bias in longitudinal 3D CT question answering.**
Built a pipeline with patient-level splits, a data-leakage audit and baseline models (slice encoder with Qwen2.5-1.5B and LoRA).

**EpiST-Former: dengue forecasting across all 64 districts of Bangladesh.**
Spatio-temporal forecasting model with baselines, ablations and significance tests. Manuscript in preparation.

**Groundwater trace-metal screening, Bengal Basin.**
Machine-learning surrogate screening for heavy-metal contamination, with conformal risk estimation. Manuscript in preparation. Code: [ground-water-](https://github.com/Mosharrof8097/ground-water-)
